# Taking part in the Kestrel pilot

**Who runs this:** [organiser's name, college or role, contact]
**What it is for:** to learn whether people find Kestrel useful enough to keep using it. [If it applies: The
results may appear in a research paper, with no names.]

## What you would do

Install Kestrel on your own laptop, use it as much or as little as suits you for two weeks, then send your
usage counts and fill in a short survey.

## What we would get

Only what you choose to send:

- **The usage counts** from Settings, "How Kestrel is used": how many times each thing was used each day, each
  feature's state, your system (Windows, Mac or Linux) and Kestrel's version. You can read all of it before
  sending.
- **Your survey answers.**
- **Anything you tell us** when asking for help.

We do not get what you type to Kestrel, your notes, documents, tasks, contacts or mail. They stay on your
laptop. Nothing is sent to us automatically.

Results use a code such as P07, not your name. Only [organiser's name] can link a code to a person, and that
list will be deleted by [date].

## What could go wrong

- This is an early version. It can give wrong answers, miss a reminder or stop working. Do not rely on it for
  anything important, and keep your own copies of important files.
- The installer is not signed, so your laptop will warn you. Install it only from the link we give you.
- It uses about 5 GB of disk, and your laptop may slow down while the model is working.
- If you turn on the internet in Kestrel and use mail, weather, news or web search, those requests go to the
  services involved (for example Google for mail). You choose this, and Settings lists each one.

## Your choices

Taking part is voluntary, and it will not affect your marks or your relationship with anyone involved. You can
stop at any time without giving a reason. Ask us to delete what you sent and we will, unless the results have
already been published without names.
