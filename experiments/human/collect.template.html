Kestrel Request Lab

Kestrel is an offline assistant for notes, tasks and reminders, built for a student research project on English and Hinglish requests. You can help in one of two ways below. Your answers are anonymous: use a short code, not your name.

Label each request with what the person wants

Pick the one label that fits; if the request asks for two different things, pick compound. Keys 1–9 and 0 pick a label, ← and → move. It takes about 20–30 minutes; your progress stays in this browser.
