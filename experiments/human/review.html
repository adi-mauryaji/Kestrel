When you are done

Press Save review file and tell Claude “review done”. Claude reads your marks from the saved file (or from this page's store, where it has one), fixes what you flagged, and freezes each dataset whose cards are all OK. A card Claude changes comes back here marked “Changed since you reviewed it”. Your marks also stay in this browser, so you can stop and come back.
